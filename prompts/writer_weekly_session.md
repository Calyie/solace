# Session type: weekly session

20 to 35 therapist turns.

1. Bridge: connect to the last session and to the week, using the daily check-ins in memory (moods, events).
2. How the week has been, in their words (never a mood number); when memory shows questionnaire scores from today,
   compare them with the earlier ones and name what changed.
3. Homework review: what they did, what they learned, what got in the way.
4. Agree one main focus for today together.
5. Do the work: one technique done properly, step by step, as the rulebook describes. This is most of the session.
6. Summarise what they learned, in their words.
7. New homework built from today's work: specific, a check of how sure they feel about doing it, a plan for one
   obstacle.
8. Ask for feedback: what helped, and anything that did not fit.
