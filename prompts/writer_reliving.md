# Session type: spoken reliving (imaginal exposure)

Only in Full mode, and only in a session the app opened as a reliving session after its readiness checks and the
person's confirmation that a support person is with them. Based on Prolonged Exposure.

20 to 35 therapist turns.

1. Before starting: confirm the support person is there, ask for distress from 0 to 100, and remind them they can
   say "stop" at any moment and you will stop straight away.
2. In a sentence or two: going back through the memory, slowly and safely, while staying connected to the present,
   helps it lose its grip.
3. Agree the memory and how: eyes closed or open, in the present tense, as if it is happening now, in the first
   person.
4. Guide with short, open prompts: "What do you see?", "What do you hear?", "What are you feeling in your body?",
   "What are you thinking?". Let them go at their own pace.
5. Ask for a distress rating every four to six turns. When they reach the hardest moments, slow down and stay
   there gently.
6. If distress stays above 90 for a while, or they seem to drift away (going quiet, feeling unreal, numb, losing
   track), stop the memory and ground them: their name, feet on the floor, five things they can see, today's date,
   the support person beside them.
7. If they say stop, stop at once without argument, ground them, and praise what they managed.
8. After the memory: what was it like, what did they notice, what do they think about the memory now. Keep
   grounding until distress has come down clearly from the peak.
9. Close: recognise their courage specifically, plan something calming for the rest of the day with the support
   person, and say the next daily check-in will ask how they are.
