# Planning the next session in a series

You are planning one session in a series of therapy app sessions with the same person. You know the person's full
profile, the week and the mood the series is aiming for that day (people improve unevenly, with setbacks), what the
therapist remembers, and the homework that was due.

Write what happened in the person's life since the last session, and a short plan for the therapist.

- events_since_last: two to five sentences in the second person ("You..."), written for the person to play. Make it
  ordinary and specific: work, people, sleep, small wins, a trigger. Say exactly how much of the homework they did
  and why. Match the target mood. Sometimes include something they will be reluctant to mention.
- plan: one to three sentences for the therapist, using only what the therapist could know from its memory plus
  sensible clinical judgement: what to follow up, and which method could fit today. Vary the methods across the
  series according to the person's needs; do not repeat the same technique every session.
- When expert methods for what this person brings are given below, plan their steps in a sensible order across the
  series: a shared understanding first, then the method step by step (for example the next rung of a ladder, not
  the first one again), then practice and keeping the gains; choose today's step from where the work has got to.
- If the therapist's memory shows the app's plan record (habits kept or missed, steps done or moved), that record is
  what really happened: describe the person's side of it (why a day slipped, what helped) and never contradict it.
- If the therapist's memory shows that risk came up recently, the plan starts with a short safety check-in, and
  builds or reviews the safety plan when it fits.
- If this is the last session of the series and things have improved, plan a relapse-prevention review: what
  helped, early warning signs, and what to do if things slip.
- After a long gap (more than a week), the person may feel awkward about coming back; the plan welcomes them back
  without blame and finds out what happened.
- Keep the calendar straight. Today's date and the last session's date are in the therapist's memory, and the
  days in between may be listed. Only describe what happened on those days. A plan step or a day that comes after
  today has not happened yet, so never report it as done, and never mention a weekend or weekday that has not come.
- homework_done: "all", "some" or "none". For the first session (intake) there is no homework: write "none", and
  describe how the person has been lately and why they are starting now.

Write in English, whatever the person's country or first language (the app is English in version 1).

Answer with JSON only: {"events_since_last": "...", "plan": "...", "homework_done": "..."}
