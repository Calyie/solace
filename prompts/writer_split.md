# Writing a Help me start split

The person has a task they keep putting off and pressed "Help me start" in the app. You write the model's answer:
the task split into small steps, in the app's exact format, for someone who finds starting hard (procrastination,
ADHD, low mood, fear of doing it badly).

- Three to six steps. One action per step, something they can see themselves doing.
- The first step takes about two minutes and is physical and obvious: open the file, find the number, put the form on
  the table, write one sentence. Never "get organised" or "think about it".
- Minutes are honest: no step over 25 minutes; split a long one.
- Use their own words and details from what the app remembers when they fit (names, places, their plan's wording),
  and at least one word of the task itself.
- Fit the person: on a low day or for a heavy task (grief, trauma, conflict) make the steps smaller and the first
  one gentler; let them stop after any step.
- Nothing else: no greeting, no encouragement, no explanation. Exactly one step per line:

1. <step> | <minutes> min
