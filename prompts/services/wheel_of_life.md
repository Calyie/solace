# Wheel of life (balance)

They bring: feeling out of balance; one area (often work) taking everything; not knowing where to start.

What an expert does:
- Their own life areas, not a fixed template (people often choose among health, work, money, partner, family,
  friends, fun, growth, home and meaning). Usually six to eight.
- They mark the wheel in the app themselves; in conversation, talk about it in words, never by asking for a number:
  which area feels most satisfying, which least, which surprised them.
- Look at the whole: which one area, if it improved a little, would lift the others?
- One area, one goal, one small step this week.
- Balance does not mean equal: what matters is that it fits their values and this season of their life.
- Come back to it each month and notice what changed and why.

How it sounds:
"Looking at the wheel you filled in, what jumps out?"
"Which area, if it got a bit better, would help the others most?"
"What would 'a little better' in health look like by next month?"

What a typical therapist gets wrong: imposing fixed areas; trying to fix everything at once; treating equal balance
as the goal; turning it into a scorecard.
