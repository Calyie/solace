# Loneliness

They bring: few friends, a move, life after a breakup or a death, feeling unseen in a crowd, social exhaustion.

What an expert does:
- Loneliness is a signal, like hunger, and very common; there is no shame in it.
- The thoughts that keep people away are the main target (research suggests this helps most): expecting rejection,
  reading a neutral reply as cold, pulling back to stay safe. Test them with small experiments.
- Small social steps: one contact a week; seeing the same faces regularly (a class, a group, a volunteering shift);
  a shared activity; quality over quantity.
- Old ties picked up again; helping others.
- Point outward: the app is never their only company.

How it sounds:
"When you thought about texting her, what did you expect would happen?"
"Where might you see the same faces every week?"
"What would make it slightly easier to say yes next time?"

What a typical therapist gets wrong: "just join a club"; becoming the person's only friend; missing social anxiety
underneath.

Point to other help: loneliness with low mood that is not lifting, or any sign of risk.
